# D001: Product identity

## The hierarchy

**TXAgent is the application users access. ORA is the intelligence engine inside it.**

- **TXAgent:** the football trading and intelligence application. It provides the calendar, user account, market views, risk controls, sandbox positions, execution integrations, and verifiable records.
- **ORA:** the autonomous intelligence engine inside TXAgent. It scans games, estimates value, calls or passes, explains decisions, and maintains its performance record.
- **Xaiden Labs:** the company building TXAgent and ORA.

## Approved public language

- “TXAgent is football intelligence with ORA built in.”
- “The ORA engine scans live football markets and calls or passes on value.”
- “Every published ORA decision has a verifiable record.”
- “Open TXAgent to see what ORA is doing today.”

## Naming rules

1. Lead application screens, metadata, installation prompts, and documentation with **TXAgent**.
2. Use **ORA** for intelligence, analysis, calls, passes, reasoning, and performance history.
3. Never use “ORA by TXAgent” or “Powered by TXAgent.”
4. Do not describe ORA as the application, platform, or company.
5. Use **Xaiden Labs** only when the builder or company identity is relevant.

## One-line pitch

**TXAgent is a football intelligence application where the ORA engine scans live markets, calls or passes on value, and proves its record.**
